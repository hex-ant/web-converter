FFmpeg WebAssembly

This site distributes @ffmpeg/core 0.12.10, an FFmpeg 5.1.4 WebAssembly build licensed under the GNU GPL 2.0 or later. The JavaScript wrapper and utilities are MIT-licensed.

  • ffmpeg.wasm 0.12.10 source
  • FFmpeg 5.1.4 source
  • Build recipes and codec dependency sources
  • GNU GPL 2.0 text

The build enables GPL mode and libx264, libx265, libvpx, libmp3lame, libtheora, libvorbis, libopus, zlib, libwebp, libfreetype, libfribidi, libass and libzimg.
